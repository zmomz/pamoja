# Editing the Pamoja site — a guide for whoever looks after content

No code needed for anything here. Log in at `https://<your-domain>/wp-admin`.

Everything that is Pamoja's lives under the **Pamoja** menu in the left sidebar (the
palm-tree icon), plus **Blog** just above it. The Overview page inside Pamoja links to
each thing you can add.

## How the site is put together

The tree is the map. Its parts lead to the four doors:

| Door | Part of the tree | What's there |
|---|---|---|
| **About** | soil, trunk, branches, roots | Why we exist · Our story · How we work together · Ethics and values — one page, four stops |
| **Events** | fruit | Upcoming, ongoing, past. Each upcoming event is a fruit on the tree |
| **Blog** | leaves | Stories from the work |
| **Engage** | seeds | Volunteer · Partner · Support, and the conversation form |

You do not edit the tree. It draws itself from the content below.

## The copy rules — five things to hold onto

They apply to everything you write, including headlines, alt text and photo captions:

1. **Newcomers are the subject, never the object.** If a sentence still makes sense with
   newcomers as people being helped, rewrite it until they do the verb.
2. **Never write** "serving/helping/supporting newcomers", "beneficiaries", "clients"
   (for community members), "participants receiving", "newcomers in need of".
3. **Write instead:** building with · working alongside · hosting · convening · teaching ·
   contributors · knowledge holders · collaborators — newcomers **who bring** X.
4. **Cut "giving newcomers a voice" / "amplifying voices" entirely.**
5. **The one exception:** under Engage → Partner, "the client" means the *institution*
   engaging us.

When numbers appear, they describe the size of a gathering, never a caseload.

## Photos: the consent rule (read this first)

Every photo in the **Media Library** has a checkbox: **Consent confirmed** — "Consent is
on file for every identifiable person in this photo." It sits in the right-hand panel
when you click a photo, next to the **Alternative text** box.

A photo appears on the site **only** when that box is ticked **and** the alt text is
filled in. Otherwise it is held back everywhere — tiles, galleries, share images — even
if the post is published. The edit screen lists any photo being held back.

- **Alt text** describes the image for someone who can't see it. The reversal test
  applies: people hosting, pouring, teaching — not being served.
- **Never publish children's faces without explicit parental consent.** When in doubt,
  leave the photo out.
- The five photographs supplied for the launch were imported with consent confirmed
  and the source noted. Untick the box on any of them to withdraw it.

## Adding an event

**Pamoja → Events → Add event.** The title is the gathering's public name; the main text
is its story. Then, in **Event details**:

- **Date or date range (as displayed)** — exactly as it should read: "October 2026",
  "Every two weeks since 2023".
- **Start date** — a real date; it sorts the lists and places upcoming events. **End
  date** is optional.
- **Location** — park, venue, neighbourhood; blank means just "Hamilton".
- **Status** — *Upcoming* shows the event as a fruit on the tree and under "Coming up"
  on the home page and Events, with a "tell me when it's announced" field. *Ongoing* is
  for a recurring gathering. *Past* is everything grown so far. *Handed on* means the
  work now belongs to someone else; choose who under **Handed on to**.
- **Video (optional)** — a YouTube or Vimeo link; it plays in place on the event page
  and marks the tile with a play button.
- **Partners and funders credited** — tick everyone who co-created, hosted or funded.
- **Pull quote** — optional, one line that carries the gathering.
- **Featured** — puts the event among the tiles under "How we've come together so far"
  on the home page. The four most recent featured events show, in their **Order**
  (right-hand panel, lower number first).
- **Branches** (right-hand panel) — tick every branch this gathering genuinely served.

**Photo gallery** (below the details): *Add photos*, pick from the library or upload,
drag to reorder. Each thumbnail shows *Ready* or what it still needs. The **Featured
image** (right-hand panel) is the tile and the share image; if there is none, the first
ready gallery photo is used.

Note: WordPress schedules a post if its *Publish* date is set in the future. For an
upcoming event, leave the publish date as today and put the real date in **Start date**.

## Writing a blog post

**Blog → Write a post.** A title, the text, and optionally a featured image (consent
rule applies) and an excerpt (used as the teaser). Publish. Posts are at `/blog/`.

## Adding a media album

**Pamoja → Media albums → Add album.** A title, an optional description, photos (same
consent rule), an optional YouTube/Vimeo link, and optionally the event it belongs to.
Albums are at `/media/` and linked from their event's page.

## Services (Engage → Partner)

**Pamoja → Services.** Each service has the same eight fixed sections. They appear as
expandable cards under Engage → Partner → "What we offer". Any paragraph in Cultural
protocols that starts with `[` is a draft note and is **not** shown. **Order** sets the
card order.

## Partners, allies, funders

**Pamoja → Partners & funders.** Name, the **contribution** (what they actually gave),
**type**, optional website. They appear under Engage → Partner → "Who we grow with" and
are credited on the event pages that name them.

## Branches

**Pamoja → Events → Branches.** The four functions — name, description and **display
order**. Don't add a fifth without changing the design.

## The site copy

**Pamoja → Site copy.** One tab per part of the site: the home page (hero, who we are,
what we've grown, coming up, seeds), the About page (page head plus the four stops), the
Engage page (page head, Volunteer, Partner, Support, the form), and the other pages.
Every passage is a field. Save one tab at a time. **Empty a field to go back to the
launch wording.**

The four About stop titles and the three Engage section titles are also the names on the
tree and in the menu, so keep them short.

Two things on the About page are waiting for you: the **source links** for the two
statistics (BDC 2024, RBC 2025) under *About · Why we exist*, and the **Volunteer**
wording under *Engage · Volunteer*, which is a draft. A paragraph starting with `[` is a
draft note and is never shown.

## Site settings

**Pamoja → Site settings.** The contact email (shown on the site and where the forms
go), the description for search and sharing, footer lines, social links (one per line as
`Label | URL`), the **land acknowledgment** (leave empty until the final wording is
confirmed — it appears in every footer once entered), the default share image, and an
optional Plausible analytics domain (no analytics load without it).

## Inquiries

**Pamoja → Inquiries.** Every message sent through the conversation form (with who they
are writing as: a neighbour, a connector or partner, an organizer), and every "tell me
when it's announced" request with the event it is about. The same message is emailed to
the contact inbox.
